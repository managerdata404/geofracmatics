About Geo Fractal Cymatics (Geofracmatics)

Geosciences is a field that explores Earth’s physical structure, processes, and history. Our research focuses on leveraging fractals and cymatics patterns to better understand and model complex geological phenomena such as:

  • Fault dynamics and tectonics
  • Earth’s surface and subsurface patterns
  • Seismic wave interactions and energy distributions

Key Concepts

Fractals in Geosciences

Fractals are intricate, self-similar patterns that emerge from simple mathematical rules repeated across scales. In geosciences, fractals are used to model:

  • Topography: Mountain ranges, river networks, and coastlines exhibit fractal-like structures.
  • Fault systems: The spatial distribution of faults and seismic events often follows fractal patterns.
  • Fracture mechanics: Understanding crack propagation in rock formations.

Cymatics Patterns

Cymatics is the study of wave patterns formed by sound vibrations on a medium. This concept is applied in geosciences to simulate:

  • Seismic wave propagation: Visualizing how seismic waves move through Earth’s layers.
  • Resonance effects: Understanding how natural frequencies impact rock formations and structures.
  • Dynamic earth processes: Analyzing energy flow in magma chambers, tectonic plates, and mantle convection.
